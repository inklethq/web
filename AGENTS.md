## Journal editorial direction

- Write articles in English, with a user story at the center: a concrete situation, what the person wants to do, and how inklet changes that moment.
- Introduce features through their benefit within that story. Include technical details only when they help the reader understand the experience or make a decision.
- Do not structure product stories around internal test runs, matching counts, logs, token usage, or implementation checklists. Do not invent customer testimonials or imply unreleased hardware is shipping.
- When replacing the featured article, keep the previous article available as a normal entry with its existing URL. Keep exactly one featured article.
